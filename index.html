About

Hi I'm Taiwo John Kehinde

I'm a Product Manager building and scaling digital products — from figuring out what to build, to getting it into the hands of real users, to making it better once it's live.

I currently work in fintech, where I build products across credit and payments. My background in product design still shapes how I work. I think deeply about the people using what we build, but I'm equally interested in the systems behind the experience — the business case, the technical constraints, and what the numbers say after launch. I can move from a customer problem to a product strategy, work through the details with engineers and designers, and stay close enough to the product to know whether what we shipped actually worked.

Lately, I've also been spending more time experimenting with AI and automation. I'm fascinated by how quickly the distance between an idea and a working product is shrinking, and I'm exploring what that means for how product teams research, prototype, build, and ship.

Outside of work, I teach design and product management to people starting out in tech. When I'm not doing that, you'll probably find me watching anime, looking for a good gospel movie or trying out a new trendy tool
